## Caveats checked

* Public GBK NS candidate is not a valid Shift-JIS oracle; byte177 decode error. Rejected instead of replacing characters or blaming an unsupported encoding on the plugin.
* Existing `.ja.bak` JSON is English. 412 array lengths agree; no assumption of Japanese originals/human authorship. Third-party pair data is a separate unique-value lexical check.
* Motto archive indices have hashed/extensionless names (zero .ks/.scn suffixes in archive-inventory.json). Gameplay payloads are unexamined; six loose initialization scripts do not cover gameplay.
* Existing XP3 patch members may be encoded/hashed-name resources. Their original payloads are compared as bytes; no invented plaintext/semantic names are assigned.
